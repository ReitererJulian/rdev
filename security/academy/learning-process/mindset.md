## Learning how to learn

>[!IMPORTANT]
>We can only get better through practice. 

There is no other way to improve our practical skills. For example, we can read 50 books about programming, and we will only understand how to read the code.

The process of "learning how to `learn`" is not easy. Most people have never truly learned how to learn effectively. For example, in school, our teachers discussed some topics with our class. First, teachers show us just one way to solve a problem and after that, they gave us exercises to practice further.

Let us take a closer look at the problem using this simple math equation:

`20 * _____ + _____ = 65535`

This equation is easy to solve, but did we think about how many different ways are there to solve it?

### Thinking outside the box

What limitations were you given for this exercise? - None.

So, why didn't you think to add more digits or to replace the given arithmetic operations?

Welcome to the hacker's way of thinking called:

"**`Outside the box`**."

Another easy example to this concept could be that our computer has stopped working.
There could be many reasons for this, as we can already imagine. It can be because the power supply is faulty, CPU issues, or maybe even the motherboard is fried.

When we have a problem, we often start by thinking about all the possible causes before trying to solve it. Many people use a process of elimination and check the most likely causes first. However, this can lead us in the wrong direction and waste a lot of time. For example, with a computer problem, people might immediately take the computer apart and check all the connections instead of considering a simple cause.

After all, the question we should be asking is:

- `Why is our computer not getting power?`

When we ask this question, we automatically form associations in our minds that have to do with the word "power ."
Because of this most will usually be focused on the power supply.

Nevertheless, let us not forget to think outside the box when we think of "power".
Because if we set ourselves the limits that it can only be our computer, we limit our options drastically. If we leave these limits, the first point of contact from the computer is the connected socket and the multiple plugs. If we followed this approach, we would see that our multi-plug had been switched off; therefore, the computer had no power to start up.

### Understanding concepts rather than individual steps

Once we are familiar with SQL injection and how it occurs, we may find the individual 
steps difficult at first. However, once we understand the overall concept, it will be easy for us to discover when a web application is vulnerable to SQL injections.

>[!IMPORTANT]
>The concept is the main focus when learning new topics.

`The simplest explanation for the best approach to penetration testing is that we work with the information we can get`

Once we understand the overall picture, adapting to the given situations and conditions is much easier. However, suppose we have only learned the individual steps. In that case, we will have difficulty adapting them to new situations because we do not understand their impact on the systems and their applications.

## Talent

There is no precise definition of talent.

Because the official definition is that talent is a skill caused by nature. 

Then let us ask the following:

- Who among the several million students on Hack The Box can naturally fly an airplane?

Starting and taking off an airplane requires considerable technical knowledge, which differs based on the type of airplane. Maybe there will be one who, with luck and without knowledge, manages to start the plane and drive with it a few meters, but taking off requires knowledge of the controls and physics. No one knows how to fly an airplane naturally without considerable training and practice.

However, we know that we have a good influence on our thought processes and thought patterns. This means we can develop and train such talent for any field we want.

We should keep in mind that we are not born with our talents/skills. A newborn baby will not suddenly start speaking in 5 different languages. Also, if we have a guitarist who has been playing guitar for years, it will be much easier for that person to learn to play bass guitar than someone who has never played a string instrument.
